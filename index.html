I am Xin Yin (Shawn; Chinese name 尹鑫, also known as 江书晚), a master's student in Control Engineering at Harbin Institute of Technology (2026–2029). I received my B.E. in Automation (自动化类) from HIT (2022–2026).

I work on vision-and-language navigation (VLN / VLN-CE), embodied AI, and multimodal agents, with a focus on robots that follow natural-language instructions. I interned at Lumos Robotics in Shenzhen and the Beijing Innovation Center of Humanoid Robotics in Beijing.
