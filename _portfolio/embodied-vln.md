This line of work studies how a robot can follow natural-language instructions in indoor scenes, including:

- Vision-and-language navigation in Habitat / VLN-CE (R2R, RxR)
- Multimodal agents that combine language reasoning with action execution
- Transfer from simulation to physical robots

Code and papers will be linked here as they are released.
